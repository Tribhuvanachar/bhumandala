Guru Parampara · Madhva (Dvaita Vedanta) Lineage

The apostolic lineage of the Madhva sampradaya — from the eternal parampara (Narayana → Brahma → … → Madhvacharya) through the four Vedanta-peetha successors and every major matha's succession: Uttaradi, Raghavendra (Mantralaya), Vyasaraja, Sripadaraja, the Udupi Ashta Mathas, the GSB Kashi & Gokarna maths, the early mathas, and the Dāsa Paramparā (Haridāsa) lineage — Sripadaraja & Vyasatirtha through Purandara & Kanaka Dasa to the 18th-c. Dasakuta of Vijaya, Gopala and Jagannatha Dasa. Each figure carries dates, Brindavana, works, contribution, and auto-computed contemporaries.
