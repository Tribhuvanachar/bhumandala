🙏 About This Project

This is part of the Sarvamoola Digitisation & Educational Project — a free, ad-free digital library of Sanskrit devotional and philosophical texts in the Madhva Sampradaya (Dvaita Vedanta) tradition, with transliteration, audio, and AI-assisted study tools, built for personal and community learning.
